## Scope and method

Reviewed all 420 existing Unit 1 and 2 questions against their scenario, key and worked reasoning: 120 in 1.A, 100 in 1.B, 100 in 2.A and 100 in 2.B. Each solution received a freshly authored, question-specific reasoning step. Existing useful working and focused misconception warnings remain. The additions explain why a procedure fits, identify the relevant reference group or event, interpret an answer or explain the information that a display or design cannot supply. They are not generated from a shared stock paragraph.

- Unit 1.A: observational units, variable types, reference populations, graph interpretation, weighted summaries, resistant statistics, linear transformations and standardized positions.
- Unit 1.B: the roles of random selection and assignment, sampling methods, sources of bias, experimental units, replication, confounding, controls, blocks, pairs and the limits of causal or population conclusions.
- Unit 2.A: joint versus conditional shares, appropriate denominators, event translation, complements, disjoint paths, changing without-replacement probabilities, independence tests and reverse conditioning.
- Unit 2.B: probability-weighted means and variances, net gains, binomial conditions and boundaries, normal areas and inverse cutoffs, expected counts, sampling distributions and the distinction between sample size and simulation repetitions.

All question IDs, prompts, scenarios, choices, answer letters, stimuli, warnings and calculator descriptors are unchanged. Unit 3 is unchanged.
